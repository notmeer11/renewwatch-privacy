RenewWatch

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026 · App: RenewWatch (Android, package com.renewwatch.app)

RenewWatch is a private trial and subscription reminder app. We do not create user accounts, we do not connect to your bank, and the subscription list you type stays on your phone.

1. Who we are

RenewWatch is published by Meer Studios. This policy describes how the Android app handles information. Contact: fazenotmeermeer@gmail.com.

2. What we store on your device

If you add a subscription or free trial, the app saves it locally on your phone, for example:

  • Name, price, billing cycle, next charge date, optional trial end date, status, and notes
  • Whether you unlocked Pro, and your reminder timing preference

This information is stored with on-device storage (not on a RenewWatch server). Uninstalling the app deletes it from that device unless you have a backup you made yourself.

3. What we do not collect

RenewWatch does not ask you to sign up. We do not collect:

  • Name, email, or phone number for a RenewWatch account
  • Bank logins, card numbers, or payment details
  • Location, contacts, photos, SMS, or microphone data
  • Advertising IDs for ads (this version has no ads)

We do not sell personal information. We do not run our own analytics SDK in this version.

4. Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders are scheduled locally on your device so we can warn you before a renewal or trial end. We do not send marketing push messages from a RenewWatch server. You can deny notification permission; the rest of the app still works.

5. Purchases (Google Play)

Optional Pro is a one-time in-app product sold through Google Play Billing, not through a RenewWatch checkout. Google processes the payment. We do not see your Google payment method.

Google may keep purchase records under Google’s own policies so Pro can be restored on the same Google account after reinstall. That purchase metadata is handled by Google, not stored on a RenewWatch backend.

6. Permissions

  • Notifications — optional, for local reminders
  • Billing — to buy or restore Pro through Google Play
  • Exact alarms / boot — so scheduled reminders can fire at the chosen time, including after reboot

These permissions are used only for reminders and Play purchases.

7. Children

RenewWatch is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Third parties

If you install from Google Play or make a purchase, Google provides the store and billing. Their handling of account, device, and payment data is described in Google’s privacy policy. RenewWatch does not operate a server that receives your subscription list.

9. Data retention and deletion

Your list remains on the device until you delete individual items, clear app storage, or uninstall. There is no RenewWatch cloud account to delete. For Play purchase history or refunds, use Google Play / your Google account.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Material changes will be reflected in a new app version when required.

11. Contact

Privacy questions: fazenotmeermeer@gmail.com

RenewWatch does not cancel third-party subscriptions for you. You cancel with each service provider. We only remind you on the dates you enter.
